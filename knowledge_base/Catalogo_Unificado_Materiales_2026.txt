Confirmation items before installation
01.The ground must be flat and should not have any depressions or 
bumps. If the surface is uneven, it needs to be leveled before 
installation.
02.Door gap space: Confirm if the thickness of the space under the 
door gap and the gap after laying the floor is sufficient.
Installation Precautions
01.Construction PreparationAfter the flooring arrives, let it rest on a 
flat surface for 24 hours.
02.Leave expansion joints. Be sure to leave a 1cm wide expansion 
joint at the wall side.
03.Partitions should be installed. In areas where the flooring 
installation length exceeds 8 meters, partitions must be done.
Usage precautions
01.Please avoid prolonged exposure to sunlight to prevent the floor 
from discoloring and warping.
02.Avoid forcing heavy or sharp objects to scrape, to prevent 
serious damage to the surface of the floor.
03.Avoid placing items at temperatures exceeding 60 degrees for 
long periods.
LAYING MODE
1/2 I-beam paving method
1/3 Method of Three Six Nine Stacking
